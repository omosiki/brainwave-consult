What is IJMB?

IJMB (Interim Joint Matriculation Board) is a nine-month Advanced Level programme moderated by Ahmadu Bello University (ABU Zaria). The programme is offered through coordinating institutions and affiliated study centres across Nigeria.

IJMB allows students to gain Direct Entry admission into 200 Level in many Nigerian universities.

Candidates must have O'level (SSCE) results in one or two sittings, and students with awaiting results can also apply.

Our Services

IJMB

The Interim Joint Matriculation Board (IJMB) programme is a Federal Government approved programme, moderated by Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. It is a one-year programme that provides candidates the opportunity to secure admission into 200 Level in most Nigerian universities.

JUPEB

The Joint Universities Preliminary Examinations Board (JUPEB) programme is a national examination body approved by the Federal Government of Nigeria. It offers candidates an opportunity to secure admission into Nigerian universities directly into 200 Level without JAMB.
