IJMB & JUPEB Pathway

A smarter, faster and more reliable route to gain university admission

IJMB Programme

IJMB is a highly recognized advanced level programme that gives students direct entry admission into 200 level in Nigerian universities without writing JAMB.

  • Duration: 9–12 months
  • Accepted nationwide
  • No JAMB stress
  • Affordable tuition

JUPEB Programme.

JUPEB is a one-year intensive programme that qualifies students for direct entry admission into both Nigerian and selected foreign universities.

  • Duration: 1 year
  • Duration: 1 year
  • International recognition
  • Direct 200 level entry
  • University moderated exams
